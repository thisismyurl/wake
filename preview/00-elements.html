Elements

Type, colour, buttons, and the slip-status pill vocabulary at a glance. DM Sans carries headings and UI, IBM Plex Sans carries reading copy and the rate sheet.

Typography: DM Sans (headings) / IBM Plex Sans (body)

Body copy runs in IBM Plex Sans at 1.65 line-height: a technical, instrument-panel humanist face chosen because it reads right for a marina's operational content: rates, hours, and slip specs, not just prose. Numerals are tabular throughout the site so a dockage rate lines up in a list the way it does on a printed rate sheet. A link looks like this inside a paragraph.
